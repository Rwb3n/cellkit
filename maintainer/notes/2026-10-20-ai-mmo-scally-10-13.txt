8. A landing's gatecheck reads the main checkout's untracked files.
   Broke: while a landing ran its step-6 gatecheck on main, the integrator wrote four research files into main's working tree. The cell-audit row ("owned by no cell") failed, so nothing was pushed. All 23 other rows had passed on the exact commit. Recovery: move the files aside, re-run only the audit row (clean), then push the checked commit.
   Becomes (structure): the root write guard refuses Write/Edit to the main checkout while the landing lock is held. Alternative (mechanism): step 6 audits the commit's tree rather than the working tree. The root hook already refuses commits during a landing, but not file writes.

9. The pause dance as the way to run one task.
   Broke: running one task meant --unpause, then run, then --pause (after it had started) about six times in one session. Once the order was wrong (pause before run), and the run had to be redone.
   Becomes (mechanism): `cellrun --cell X --one` takes exactly one queued message, then leaves the cell paused. It was on our list behind a freeze that has now lifted.

Reviewing the task mail before sending caught three more blockers: a publish command that fails restore, a rebuild that would silently pass hidden state through, and scripts that couldn't exercise the field under test.
   Becomes (mechanism): the cell's own session runs a fresh reviewer before it replies, and the reply carries the verdict. The integrator also reviews the mail before sending, as a step in the send command rather than a habit. A review verdict as a landing precondition is already on our list.
